TIER 1 — BEGINNER
The Starter Experience — Simplicity, Trust, Action
yourwebsite.com/start

Who This Person Is

  • Local business owner — plumber, dentist, restaurant, contractor
  • Relies on word-of-mouth or walk-in traffic
  • Intimidated by technology and digital terms
  • Core need: more phone calls, bookings, customers
  • Budget-sensitive, needs to see simple ROI math
  • Likely been burned before by ads or a website that didn't work

Psychological Profile

This person is skeptical and overwhelmed. Your job is NOT to sell them on digital marketing — it's to earn trust and make complexity feel simple.


Don't sell them "marketing." Sell them "more customers." They don't care how — they want the result.

Page Copy — Section by Section

HERO HEADLINE (H1)
"Get More Customers.
Without the Confusion."
We help local businesses like yours get a steady flow of new customers from the internet — without you needing to learn anything technical. We handle everything.
PROBLEM SECTION HEADLINE
"Does Any of This Sound Familiar?"
• You're great at what you do — but new customers are hard to find
• You have no idea where to start with the internet
• You tried ads before and just burned money
• Your competitors seem to be everywhere online and you're not
• You don't have time to learn all this — you have a business to run

SOLUTION: Simple 4-Step Flow (Visual)

Present as horizontal steps with icons and connecting arrows. Mobile: vertical stack.

Step 1 — We build you a professional website that actually works
Step 2 — We run ads on Facebook, Instagram & Google to bring people to you
Step 3 — We capture their interest and qualify them (ready-to-buy only)
Step 4 — We deliver you leads — real people, ready to become customers
PRIMARY CTA
"Start Getting More Customers →
Book a Free 20-Min Call"
No obligation. No tech talk. Just a simple conversation about your business.

✅ Words TO Use

  • customers, calls, bookings
  • people finding you
  • results, simple, easy
  • we handle everything
  • your business, for you
  • ready to buy, interested people

🚫 Words to NEVER Use

  • funnel, conversion rate, pixel
  • CPC, ROAS, CPM, CTR
  • automation, CRM, API
  • retargeting, attribution
  • lead nurturing, lifecycle
  • omnichannel, touchpoints

TIER 2 — INTERMEDIATE
The Growth Experience — Methodology, Proof, System
yourwebsite.com/grow

Who This Person Is

  • Has a website, maybe running basic ads
  • Understands leads, conversions, Facebook ads basics
  • Frustrated with inconsistent or underperforming results
  • In comparison mode — evaluating agencies
  • Thinking about CRM, automation for the first time
  • Will invest if ROI is clear and demonstrable

Psychological Profile

Analytical and frustrated. They've invested in marketing without the expected returns. They're comparing agencies.


Don't sell them "why digital marketing matters." Sell them WHY YOUR SYSTEM is better than what they have now. Lead with methodology and proof.

HERO HEADLINE (H1)
"Your Ads Are Running.
Your Pipeline Isn't."
Most businesses at your stage are generating interest — but losing leads in the gap between attention and conversion. We close that gap with a full-funnel system: ads, landing pages, lead qualification, nurturing, and CRM integration.
PROBLEM SECTION
"The Growth Plateau is Real. Here's What's Causing It."
• Leads come in but don't convert — no nurture system in place
• Ad spend is rising but cost-per-lead isn't improving
• Running Facebook AND Google but they aren't coordinated
• No clear attribution — you don't know which channel works
• Team manually follows up — inefficient and inconsistent

Show as an animated SVG funnel. Each layer clickable to expand detail.

Layer 1 — Paid Traffic: Meta Ads + Google Ads coordinated strategy

Layer 3 — Lead Qualification: Smart forms + call qualification flows
Layer 4 — Lead Nurturing: Email sequences, SMS, retargeting
Layer 5 — CRM Integration: Leads auto-enter pipeline, nothing slips

The Scale Experience — Depth, Partnership, Custom Infrastructure
yourwebsite.com/scale

Who This Person Is

  • Marketing director or founder, 5+ figures/month revenue
  • Speaks: ROAS, CAC, LTV, MQL/SQL, API, AI agents
  • Has functioning marketing — wants to upgrade capability
  • Evaluating partners on depth, custom solutions, culture fit

Don't pitch them. Consult them. Your homepage should feel like the first page of a strategy document, not a sales brochure.

Technical Specifications

Developer Requirements

Everything your developer needs to build this correctly the first time.

🟢 Beginner /start

Color: Warm Green #1A7A3C

Tone: Friendly, large body text

Imagery: Real people, local businesses

CTA: Large, simple, low friction

Proof: Testimonials, before/after

Form: 4 fields max

🔵 Intermediate /grow

Color: Trust Blue #1A56FF

Tone: Professional, data-forward

Imagery: Dashboards, results, teams

CTA: Audit/diagnostic, medium friction

Proof: Case studies with metrics

Form: 6 fields, budget range

🟡 Pro /scale

Color: Premium Gold #B07800

Tone: Editorial, sophisticated

Imagery: Abstract, architectural

CTA: Strategy session, high intent

Proof: Enterprise case studies

Form: 6 fields, revenue range

📊

Conversion Tracking: Each tier needs independent GA4 events. Track: page visit, scroll depth (25/50/75/100%), CTA click, form start, form submit, booking complete. Pass tier source as UTM parameter to CRM on every lead.

📱

Mobile Requirements: All pages 100% mobile-first. Test on iPhone SE as baseline. Hero fully readable without scroll. CTAs thumb-reachable (bottom 60%). Load time under 2 seconds on 4G.

🔗

Lead Routing: All forms connect to CRM with tier tag. Email notification to sales includes tier context. Beginner leads get nurture sequence. Intermediate get audit booking. Pro get strategy call booking.

Build Roadmap

6-week build-and-launch plan with clear deliverables per phase.

1
Foundation — Week 1–2
Finalize copy for all tiers · Design system (colors, fonts, components per tier) · Homepage router design · Mobile-first wireframes all three pages
2
Build — Week 3–4
Develop all three tier pages · CRM form integration with tier tagging · Conversion tracking per tier · Homepage self-selection routing with quiz option
3
Optimize — Week 5–6
QA across devices and browsers · A/B testing setup on hero headlines · Load speed optimization · Soft traffic test before full launch
4
Scale — Month 2+
Analyze conversion by tier · Add video testimonials to highest-converting tier · Blog/resources section per tier language · Industry-specific landing pages (dentists, contractors, etc.)

⚠️ Non-Negotiable Rules (Developer + Copywriter)

✓Every page has ONE primary CTA. One. Not three options.
✓Beginner page: zero jargon. Have a non-marketer read and flag anything unclear.
✓Pro page: zero fluffy claims. Every sentence defensible and specific.
✓CTA above the fold on every single page.
✓Every testimonial must include specific numbers. "It worked great" is NOT proof.
✓Design mobile-first. Desktop is the secondary consideration.
✓When sending link to a prospect, send the tier-matched URL, not the homepage.
